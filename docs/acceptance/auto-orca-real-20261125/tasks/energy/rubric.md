# Fixed geometries of gas-phase water

The comparison is between ground-state electronic energies at the two supplied geometries, evaluated in separate calculations. Each supplied geometry fixes the elements, their Cartesian coordinates in Å and the neutral singlet state. The method is PBE0/def2-SVP with TightSCF and ORCA gas-phase defaults. The reference is run independently from `reference/A.inp` and `reference/B.inp` before grading. Save these outputs as `A.out` and `B.out` in the reference directory. Their absolute energies and ordering are calculated from the raw output at grade time. The solver receives only `instruction.md` and `inputs/`.

All checks must pass. For each of A and B, compare the input submitted by the solver with the input echoed by ORCA, the printed three-atom geometry with its matching frozen XYZ, and the actual electronic model, charge, multiplicity and SCF criteria with the requested state. The raw output must contain a single converged SCF, one final single-point energy and normal termination in that order. Two calculations must have distinct inputs and outputs. The fresh trial workspace and executor launch evidence establish provenance externally. The grader independently validates chemical and numerical evidence. It never starts ORCA.

Printed Cartesian distances are compared within 0.000004 Å, allowing rigid coordinate transforms and equivalent hydrogens. Compare each submitted absolute energy with its raw output (0.00000002 Eh). Compare each raw energy with its independent reference (0.000002 Eh). Compare the signed reported difference with the difference between unrounded raw energy lines (0.00000003 Eh and 0.003 kJ/mol) and with the independent reference difference (0.000004 Eh and 0.012 kJ/mol). The conversion factor is 2625.49964 kJ mol⁻¹ Eh⁻¹. The lower-energy geometry must agree with the independent reference. Tests compare absolute errors at the stated tolerances.

The actual TightSCF thresholds are read from the SCF settings table. The grader checks the printed TolE, TolG, TolX and TolErr at the TightSCF values or tighter. See the [ORCA 6.1 SCF manual](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/essentialelements/scf.html).
